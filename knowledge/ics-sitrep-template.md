# Situation Report (SITREP) Template

Owning section: Planning.
Cadence: once per operational period, plus on any significant change.

## 1. Header

- Incident name and number
- Operational period (number, start, end)
- Report time
- Prepared by (section and role)
- Incident Commander

## 2. Current situation

Two to four sentences of plain language. What is happening now, not what
happened since activation. A reader who has been off shift for twelve hours
should be able to re-enter the incident from this paragraph alone.

State explicitly:

- What changed since the last report.
- What is trending worse.
- What is trending better.

## 3. Incident objectives status

For each objective carried in the incident action plan, report one of:
**on track**, **at risk**, or **not met**, with one line of evidence.

Do not report an objective as on track without evidence. "No reports received"
is not evidence of success; it is a gap, and it belongs in section 6.

## 4. Resource status

- Committed resources by section.
- Resources requested and not yet filled, with the shortfall quantity.
- Sites below reorder point.
- Any resource that will become the limiting factor in the next operational
  period.

## 5. Health and safety

Casualty or presentation counts by category and sector. Staff welfare issues,
including rotation compliance for outdoor teams.

## 6. Information gaps and uncertainty

**This section is mandatory and may not be left blank.** List what is not
known, how it affects decisions, and who is tasked with closing the gap.

A situation report that expresses no uncertainty is either describing a very
simple incident or is wrong. State confidence plainly: where a number is an
estimate, say so and say how it was derived.

## 7. Next operational period

Planned actions, anticipated resource needs, and any decision the Incident
Commander is being asked to make.

## 8. Attachments

Maps, resource tables, and the current incident action plan.
